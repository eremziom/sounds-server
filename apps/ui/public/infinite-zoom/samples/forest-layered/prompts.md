# Built-in image_gen prompt set

Three built-in calls, no CLI/API fallback. The background request used
`transparent_background:false`; the two overlays referenced `plate.png` and used
`transparent_background:true`. Sources are copied unchanged into this package.
The requested 3840×2160 background size was **not delivered**; actual dimensions
are recorded in `production.json`. The package does not claim native 4K output.

## Plate

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: opaque background CLEAN PLATE for a layered Infinite Zoom forest artwork, landscape 16:9, requested resolution 3840x2160. Create a finely painted cinematic enchanted woodland stream at blue hour, coherent luminous teal water reflecting muted violet and warm amber bioluminescent mushrooms on mossy banks. Dense old twisting trees arch over a distant luminous clearing. A winding broad stream comes from the center distance, bends toward the left-middle then right and fills much of the lower middle foreground. Organic detailed ferns and rocks at bank edges, painterly high-detail textures, natural depth, restrained tasteful glow. Composition for animation: leave open mossy rocks/earth patches in the near lower-left and lower-right for separately authored matching foreground fern clusters; no large near foreground plants crossing these open patches. Riverbanks cleanly readable, no foreground objects crossing central water. Do NOT add fog or mist yet; those are a separate layer. The whole picture must be a beautiful complete environment including detailed water and complete ground behind future moving leaves. Full bleed picture, no frame, no text, no interface, no labels, no geometric ornaments. Keep foreground rooted rocks in lower corners and continuous terrain. This is an actual scenic painting, not an asset sheet. Save resulting image file so it can be used in the local project.

## Foliage

Use case: compositing. Image 1 is the exact registration and lighting reference for a layered woodland artwork. Produce ONLY a separate foreground vegetation layer on GENUINELY TRANSPARENT background, with exactly the same full landscape canvas, 16:9 frame, camera and pixel positions as Image 1. Do not repaint the background. Place two beautifully painted natural fern clusters rooted in the empty near-bank earth patches: one at lower left occupying normalized x=.06 to .28 and y=.57 to .94, its stems rooted near x=.18,y=.91; one at lower right x=.74 to .96,y=.59 to .95 with stems rooted near x=.88,y=.93. Keep both clusters fully inside the full image boundaries, with generous transparent margins and the whole middle .30.. .72 completely transparent. Each cluster has many irregular curling fronds and finely serrated individual leaflets with different lengths and orientations, dark natural blue-green undersides, mossy teal color, subtle amber reflected light from right and cool stream reflections. Match the dense painterly brushwork, fine texture, perspective and lighting of the reference EXACTLY so they look rooted in this specific forest. Include tiny organic grasses and a few moss sprigs around each root, but NO solid ground, no river, no trees, no sky, no other objects, no border, no labels. No smooth vector leaves, no geometric fern symbols, no icons, no asset sheet, no detached floating objects. Keep actual alpha transparency everywhere outside the detailed foliage. Output full registration frame, do not crop/center/enlarge the two plants.

## Mist

Use case: compositing. Image 1 is the registration and lighting reference, NOT background to reproduce. Create ONE FULL-FRAME TRANSPARENT OVERLAY of natural thin river mist matching the exact landscape composition and viewpoint of Image 1. Keep same canvas aspect 16:9. Paint two irregular broken wisps of cool pale blue-grey translucent mist hovering closely above the river, mainly normalized x .30 to .78, y .44 to .70, and one faint wisp at x .47 to .67,y .38 to .48. Lots of delicate curling asymmetric tendrils and wispy filaments, feathered edges, varied density, realistic airy turbulent volumes, painterly fine texture. The mist must reveal the underlying water through its alpha, maximum opacity roughly 20 to 40 percent, no opaque white patches. Warm amber tinges on right edges, teal-blue lighting from water below. Everything outside wisps genuinely alpha transparent. NO forest, no ground, no rocks, no water surface, no mushrooms, no foliage, no stars, no particles, no flat filled ovals, no vector strokes, no rectangles, no geometric shapes, no labels. Do not crop to the mist; keep full scene registration with transparent margins especially top .35 and bottom .25. The purpose is a subtle integral atmosphere layer that blends naturally into the supplied painted river scene.
