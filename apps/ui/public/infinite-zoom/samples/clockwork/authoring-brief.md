**version 1.0**. Its eight rules apply regardless of subject. The canonical rules

## Clockwork instantiation

- Composition: dark watch-like machine, dense technical structures, black steel
  and engraved antique gold. Straight top-down camera preserves round axles.
- Independent groups: five rigid wheel rotors. Fixed groups: backing, bridges,
  screws, bearings and casing. Illumination has its own protected-region mask.
- Motion: continuous opposite-direction spins with fixed authored pivots and a
  common speed control. No wave/bend/flow deformation is applied to metal.
- Complete backing: the five wells were generated empty before wheel cutouts,
  eliminating baked duplicates and supporting actual openings between spokes.
- Matte roles: original cleaned alpha defines tooth/spoke/hole silhouettes; radial
  bounds constrain travel; fixed axle caps are a separate occlusion layer. Pulse
  selects three small bearing interiors. Glint influence intersects actual bright
  source pixels with small reflection footprints, and follows each moving rotor.
  Dark metal and all unselected regions must retain their original brightness.
- Rejection/correction: first cutout's pale residual alpha was not accepted. A
  targeted background-extraction edit produced clear openings on black.
- Review evidence: fixed-pivot unit tests, actual per-asset hole transparency,
  full-turn math, unchanged bearing pixels, freeze/resume, studio-state return,
  context recovery, two aspect ratios and actual application playback.
- Limits: artistic driven mechanism, not a CAD/contact simulation; not every
  background microgear is separated. Native 4K delivery is not claimed.
