## composition
Dark alien garden and mossed stone gateway at right, thick foreground pillar near x=68%; small compact cyan drone at x=25%, y=45%, width 10%; open middle flight space.

## motionPlan
Separate drone visibility and lamp layers; 24-second curved left-to-right flight behind the right pillar, tangent rotation about measured mask centroid; inpaint its entire revealed backing. No searchlight beam, trail or baked glow.

## lightingPlan
Many dozens of fine stone fissures and leaf-vein bands across three garden groups; saturated cyan-magenta-amber gradients along material, dark gaps preserved; distinct visibility, influence and delay maps, staggered local impulses and bass binding.

## blackoutPlan
Opaque black coverage; independently animated garden details and drone lamps remain visible from the start while environment and hull reach zero opacity. Smooth eight-second reveal and return; pause and zoom QA required.

## qaPlan
Inspect mask contours, inpainted backing, full curved flight, pillar occlusion, color cycles/propagation, blackout/reveal shadows, zoom, pause/reset and portrait/wide coverage. Record motion and uncertain boundaries; no still-only acceptance.

## Production and acceptance
All source images, masks, maps and the manifest were produced by registered API jobs. No output pixels or manifest were hand-edited. Source images are 1024x576; the 3840x2160 canvas is a coordinate system, not native 4K. Motion/zoom and visual acceptance are recorded separately by QA.
